The Crawler is an extensible edge computing platform that makes it remarkably easy to capture, evaluate, and forward all relevant data directly at the machine.

Gain not only quick and deep insights into the operation of your equipment and processes, but also benefit from advanced features such as digital documentation, machine learning, and cloud connectivity.

---
## Creating added value.

| The advantage of the Crawler lies in the fusion of control technology, gateway technology, an integrated app concept, and an easy-to-use web user interface.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Thanks to its modular, extensible concept, both small use cases and complex digitalization solutions encompassing an entire production facility can be implemented quickly and individually. The Crawler is therefore an ideal enabler and starting point for digitizing your manufacturing processes.                                                                    |
| The Crawler already includes pre-installed, ready-to-use features such as connectivity to SIEMENS controllers and Modbus devices. It simultaneously allows expansion with additional customized functions such as specific connectors, condition monitoring, or load management. It is adaptable and extensible for individual applications and deployment scenarios. |
