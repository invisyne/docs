## Overview

Visualizations are organized in tabs. Good names make it easy to find your data again.

![](../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-tabs-1.png)

Click the three dots next to a tab to:

- Duplicate it
- Rename it
- Delete it

To add a tab, click the **+** sign at the end of the tab list.

## Reorganizing tabs

To reorganize tabs, drag and drop a tab to the desired place.

![](../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-tabs-2.gif)
