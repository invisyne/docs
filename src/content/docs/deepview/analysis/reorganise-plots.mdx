To reorganize plots, grab the arrows icon at the top-left of a plot and drag it to the desired position.

![](../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-reorganise-plots-1.png)

Here's an example of reorganizing plots:

![](../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-reorganise-plots-2.gif)

You can also make plots wider or smaller by dragging their borders.

![](../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-reorganise-plots-3.gif)


## Copy, paste and duplicate

- Click **duplicate** to copy a tab or plot within the same project.
- Copy and paste tabs between projects using the tab's settings menu.
- Copy and paste plots with the copy button in the plot toolbar, then the paste button at the top of the project.
