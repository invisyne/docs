## Limits

Limits are the parts of the y-axis that Deepview visualizes. There are two limit types: the **min limit** and the **max limit**.

Deepview visualizes the data points within the min and max limit. The min and max limit can be set for every signal separately.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-limits-and-stacking-1.png)

## Stacking

Each signal group also has a **position** on a plot, which you can change.

If you select one signal or a signal group, you can see drag handles on the axis. These allow you to change the position of your signal. Playing with positions allows you to make visually clean plots.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-limits-and-stacking-2.gif)

By default, in Time Series plots, all signals and signal groups added to a plot are added with their limits aligned to the top and bottom of the plot. This means the graphs are stacked over one another.

If you want to automatically stack the graphs on top of each other, click the **Stack All Signals** button in the top bar.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-limits-and-stacking-3.png)

After clicking the **Stack All Signals** button, the signals are stacked over one another in the plot.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-limits-and-stacking-4.gif)

## Linking limits

**Link** multiple signals if you want their limits and positions to always be the same. This turns these signals into a signal group (see [Signal Settings](/deepview/analysis/plot-types/time-series/signal-settings)).

Linked signals share the same y-axis. Select multiple signals (<kbd>ctrl/cmd</kbd> + left mouse click) and click the link button.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-limits-and-stacking-5.png)
