Cursors show the value of visualized signals at a specific point on the time axis. Add a second cursor to calculate the difference, minimum, maximum, and mean between the two points.

## Placing a primary cursor

Click the point in the time series plot where you want the cursor.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-cursors-1.png)

## Placing a secondary cursor

Hover over the plot and press <kbd>Space</kbd>, or click **Enable Secondary Cursor** in the toolbar above the plot. Press <kbd>Escape</kbd> or click the button again to remove it.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-cursors-2.png)

## Calculations between cursors

Results appear to the left of the plot, between the primary cursor (solid line) and the secondary cursor (dotted line).

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-cursors-3.png)
