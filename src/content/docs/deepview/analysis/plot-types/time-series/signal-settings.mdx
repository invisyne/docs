- Change the style of the signals.

- Change the limits of the signals in the group (the minimum and maximum of the y-axis). Changing the limits applies to the entire group.

{/* Screenshot: changing the limits of a signal group */}

- Reset the limits for the group, or ungroup the signals.

{/* Screenshot: reset limits or ungroup signals options */}

- When selecting an individual signal, you can also change its color.

{/* Screenshot: changing the color of an individual signal */}

- Hide a signal from the plot while keeping it visible in the cursor values table.

{/* Screenshot: hiding a signal from the plot */}

## Show or hide signal y-axes

By default, if you add multiple signals to a plot that don't all belong to the same group (or don't share the same limits), the multiple y-axes are hidden.

If you want to show these axes again, click the **Show all axes** button in the toolbar.
