---
title: "Signal Settings"
description: "Change the style, limits, color, and visibility of signals in a Time Series plot."
sidebar:
  order: 5
---

When selecting a signal, or a signal group, you can change how it appears in the plot.

- Change the style of the signals.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-signal-settings-1.png)

- Change the limits of the signals in the group (the minimum and maximum of the y-axis). Changing the limits applies to the entire group.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-signal-settings-2.png)

- Reset the limits for the group, or ungroup the signals.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-signal-settings-3.png)

- When selecting an individual signal, you can also change its color.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-signal-settings-4.png)

- Hide a signal from the plot while keeping it visible in the cursor values table.

![](../../../../../../assets/images/deepview/deepview-analysis-plot-types-time-series-signal-settings-5.png)
