## Y-axis modes

The **Axis mode** setting in the plot settings switches between two modes:

- **Simple** (default) — all signals are grouped under one y-axis that zooms dynamically to the data in view.
- **Advanced** — unlocks the other y-axis settings.

Clicking **Stack all signals**, or the unstack button next to it in the toolbar, also switches to Advanced.

## Plot axes settings

Axes settings are in the plot's settings:

- **Axis mode** — **Simple** or **Advanced**
- **Autoscale axes** — the y-axis of each signal auto-scales to the data currently in view
- **Show all X axes** — show the x-axis of every data set instead of just the first (only available when comparing data sets)
- **Show all Y axes** — show the y-axis for every linked signal group

## X-axis modes

Depending on your data, the x-axis shows time in one of two modes:

- **Absolute time** — if the data contains real timestamps, they are shown in your browser's local time zone by default. Click the globe icon at the top-right to choose a different time zone.
- **Relative time** — click the stopwatch button to show seconds since the start of the data instead. The data then starts at 0 s.
