## General

Axes settings are in the plot's settings:

- **Autoscale axes** — rescale each signal's limits on zoom to fit its position on the plot
- **Show all X axes** — show the x-axis of every data set being compared (only available when comparing data sets)
- **Show all Y axes** — show the y-axis for every linked signal group

## Time zone for the x-axis

Epoch timestamps are shown in your browser's local time zone by default. Use the **time zone** button at the top-right of the visualization to choose a different one.
