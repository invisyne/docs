Once a data set is loaded into your project, select the signals you want to visualize from the signal list on the left side of the screen.

{/* Screenshot: signal list on the left side of the screen */}

By default, the signal list shows a data set's raw signals. Toggle the switch at the top of the list to see its functions instead.

{/* Screenshot: signals/functions toggle at the top of the signal list */}

## Signal metadata

Hover over a signal name to see its metadata. See [Database](/deepview/database#signal-metadata) for how metadata is configured.

{/* Screenshot: metadata tooltip on hover */}

## Add signals to a plot

Double-click a signal, or drag and drop it onto a plot.

{/* Screenshot: dragging a signal onto a plot */}

Use the search box to find a signal by fuzzy search across all available signals — typos and case differences (e.g. "t" vs. "T") still match.

## Signal list settings

Click the settings icon at the top-right of the signal list to:

1. Turn alphabetical sorting on or off
2. Show or hide signal units
3. Group signals, and choose the separator character used for grouping
