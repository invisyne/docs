## Why Deepview

Analyzing high-frequency test and measurement data usually means engineers writing one-off scripts to load a file, plot a few signals, and repeat the process by hand for every new question. Deepview replaces that with a web-based workspace built specifically for time-series data: connect once, then explore interactively — no re-running scripts for every new question.

Deepview reads data directly from the databases behind Hub, so there is no separate export or file-handling step between recording a signal on the Edge device and analyzing it.

## Who Deepview is for

Deepview is built for engineers and service teams working with sensor data from industrial machines — commissioning engineers validating a new installation, service technicians diagnosing a fault, or engineering leads comparing performance across a fleet of machines.

## Frequently asked questions

#### What data sources does Deepview support?

Deepview connects to the time-series and asset data managed by Hub. There is nothing to configure — your data is already connected when you open Deepview.

#### Can I combine data from multiple machines in one view?

Yes — Deepview is designed to let you bring signals from several Edge devices into the same analysis, so you can compare an entire installation rather than one machine at a time.

#### Does Deepview use AI?

Deepview includes AI-assisted features, such as automatically suggesting visualizations based on what you are looking at and answering questions about your data directly. We continue to expand these capabilities.
