You can update a device's metadata at any time from the Devices list.

<Steps>
1. **Go to Devices** — Select **Devices** in the top navigation bar.

2. **Open the device** — Click on the row of the device you want to edit. The device detail panel opens.

3. **Click Edit** — Click the **Edit** button in the detail panel to enable editing mode.

4. **Update the fields** — Modify any combination of: **Name**, **Location**, **Order number**, **Delivery date**, or **Notes**.

5. **Save your changes** — Click **Save** to apply the changes. The updated information is reflected immediately in the device list.
</Steps>
