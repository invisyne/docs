:::note
This action requires an **Admin** role.
:::

Invite team members to Hub by sending them a credentials email directly from the Users page.

<Steps>
1. **Go to Users** — Click **Users** in the top navigation bar.

2. **Click the + button** — Click the **+** button to open the invite dialog.

3. **Enter the email address** — Type the new user's email address in the input field.

4. **Select a role** — Choose either **Admin** (full access, including user management) or **User** (standard access to devices and software).

5. **Confirm the invite** — Click **Invite**. The new user receives an email with their login credentials and a link to access Hub.
</Steps>
