After a device is registered in Hub, you can enrich its profile with additional information to make it easier to identify and manage in the field.

<Steps>
1. **Go to Devices** — Click **Devices** in the top navigation bar.

2. **Select the device** — Click the row of the device you want to update. The device detail panel opens on the right.

3. **Click Edit** — Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) in the detail panel to enable editing mode.

4. **Fill in the fields** — Add or update any of the following:

   | Field | Description |
   |---|---|
   | **Name** | A recognisable label for the device |
   | **Location** | Where the device is physically installed |
   | **Address** | Street address of the installation site |
   | **Contact** | Responsible person or team for this device |

5. **Save** — Click **Save**. The information is saved immediately and appears in the device list.
</Steps>
