Firmware files for Edge (Crawler) devices are available directly in Hub under the Software section.

<Steps>
1. **Go to Software** — Click **Software** in the top navigation bar.

2. **Find the desired version** — Under the **Crawler** section, browse the list of available firmware versions. Versions are listed newest-first.

3. **Download** — Click the **Download** button next to the version you want. The firmware file is saved to your browser's default download location.
</Steps>
