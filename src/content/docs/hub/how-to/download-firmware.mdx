Firmware files for Edge devices are available directly in Hub under the Software section.

<Steps>
1. **Go to Software** — Click **Software** in the top navigation bar.

2. **Find the desired version** — Under the **Crawler** section, browse the list of available firmware versions. Versions are listed newest-first.

3. **Open the detail page** — Click on the version entry to open its detail page. Here you will find release notes and further information about the release.

4. **Download** — In the detail page, click the **Download** button to download the firmware file. It is saved to your browser's default download location.
</Steps>
