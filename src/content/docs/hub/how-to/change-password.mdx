title: "Change Your Password"
description: "How to update your Hub account password from Account Settings."
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';


You can change your password at any time from your account settings.

<Steps>
1. **Open Account Settings** — Click your profile icon in the top-right corner of the navigation bar, then select **Account Settings**.

2. **Go to the Security section** — Scroll down to the **Security** section on the settings page.

3. **Click Edit Password** — Click the **Edit Password** button to expand the password change form.

4. **Enter your new password** — Type your new password in the input field. Choose a strong password that you do not use elsewhere.

5. **Save** — Click **Save**. Your password is updated immediately.
</Steps>
