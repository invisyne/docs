The latest Crawler.Companion app installers are hosted in Hub and can be downloaded at any time.

<Steps>
1. **Go to Software** — Click **Software** in the top navigation bar.

2. **Find the desired version** — Under the **Companion** section, browse the list of available versions. Versions are listed newest-first.

3. **Download** — Click the **Download** button next to the version you want. The installer is saved to your browser's default download location.
</Steps>
