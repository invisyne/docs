The **Devices** section is the central view in Hub. It lists all Edge devices registered in your organization and lets you view and edit their details.

## Device List

After logging in, you land directly on the device list showing all Edge devices registered in your organization.

## Device Detail Panel

Click any row in the Devices panel of Hub — a detail panel opens on the right side of the screen.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={devicesDetailImg.src} alt="Hub device detail panel showing device information" />
</div>

The panel contains the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Serial Number** | Unique device identifier (read-only) |
| **Name** | User-defined label (editable) |
| **Type** | Device model (read-only) |
| **Location** | Physical installation site (editable) |
| **Firmware (Delivery)** | Firmware version as shipped (read-only) |
| **Order Number** | Internal reference number (read-only) |
| **Delivered On** | Date the device was delivered (read-only) |
| **Notes** | Free-text field for additional information (editable) |

## Editing a Device

<Steps>
1. Click a device row to open the detail panel.

2. Click the **Edit** button to enable editing mode.

3. Update any of the following fields: **Name**, **Location**, or **Notes**.

4. Click **Save** to apply your changes.
</Steps>

:::note
All fields except **Name**, **Location**, and **Notes** are read-only and cannot be edited manually.
:::
