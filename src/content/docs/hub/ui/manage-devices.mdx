The **Devices** section is the central view in Hub. It lists all Edge (Crawler) devices registered in your organization and lets you view and edit their details.

## Device List

After logging in, you land directly on the device list. Each row shows:

<div class="field-list">
  <div class="field-item"><span class="field-label">Serial Number</span><span class="field-desc">Unique device identifier</span></div>
  <div class="field-item"><span class="field-label">Name</span><span class="field-desc">User-defined label</span></div>
  <div class="field-item"><span class="field-label">Type</span><span class="field-desc">Device model (e.g., Wago 752-9400)</span></div>
  <div class="field-item"><span class="field-label">Location</span><span class="field-desc">Installation site</span></div>
  <div class="field-item"><span class="field-label">Firmware</span><span class="field-desc">Installed firmware version</span></div>
</div>

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src="/images/hub/hub-devices.png" alt="Device list showing all registered Edge (Crawler) devices" />
</div>

Use the **search field** in the top right to filter by any column, or click a column header to sort. See [Filter and search](/hub/ui/filter-search/) for details.

## Editing a Device

<Steps>
1. Click any row in the device list to open the detail panel.
2. The panel shows all device information on the right side of the screen.
3. Click into any editable field — **Name**, **Location**, **Order Number**, **Delivery Date**, or **Notes** — and make your changes.
4. Your changes are saved automatically.
</Steps>
