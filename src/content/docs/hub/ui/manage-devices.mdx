The **Devices** section is the central view in Hub. It lists all Edge devices registered in your organization and lets you view and edit their details.

## Device List

After logging in, you land directly on the device list showing all Edge devices registered in your organization.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={devicesImg.src} alt="Device list showing all registered Edge devices" />
</div>

Use the **search field** in the top right to filter by any column, or click a column header to sort.

## Editing a Device

<Steps>
1. Click a device row to open the detail panel.

2. Click the **Edit** button to enable editing mode.

3. Update any of the following fields: **Name**, **Address**, **Contact**, **Location**, **Order Number**, **Delivery Date**, or **Notes**.

4. Click **Save** to apply your changes.
</Steps>

:::note
Serial number, MAC address, firmware version, and status are read-only and cannot be edited manually.
:::
