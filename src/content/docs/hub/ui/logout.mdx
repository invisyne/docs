Your profile menu is always accessible in the top-right corner of the interface. It shows your initials and gives you quick access to account settings and logout.

## Signing Out

<Steps>
1. Click your **profile icon** (showing your initials) in the top-right corner.

2. A dropdown menu opens with two options: **Account Settings** and **Logout**.

3. Click **Logout** to sign out and return to the login page.
</Steps>

  <img src={profileImg.src} alt="Profile menu open with Account Settings and Logout options" />

## Session Timeout

Hub automatically logs you out after a period of inactivity to protect your account. When this happens, you are redirected to the login page. Log back in with your usual credentials to continue.

:::note
On shared or public computers, always log out manually after each session rather than relying on the automatic timeout.
:::

## Help

You can access the documentation at any time via the **Help** link in the header menu. It opens the Invisyne Docs site in a new tab.
