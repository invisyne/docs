## Overview

Navigate to **Users** in the top navigation bar to see all accounts in your organization, including their name, email address, role, and status.

## Inviting a New User

User management is handled by administrators. They can create new user accounts, assign roles, and manage existing accounts.

<Steps>
1. Click **Invite User** to open the invitation form.

2. Enter the new user's email address and assign a role — **User** or **Admin**.

3. Click **Send Invitation**. The new user receives an email with their login credentials.
</Steps>

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={createUserImg.src} alt="Hub invite user form with email and role fields" />
</div>

## Deactivating a User

To deactivate an account, open the user entry and click **Deactivate**. The user loses access immediately. Deactivated accounts can be reactivated at any time.
