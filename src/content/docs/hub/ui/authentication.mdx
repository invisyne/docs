## Account Setup

Access to Hub is by invitation only — there is no self-registration. An administrator creates your account and sends you your login credentials (email address and initial password) by email. You can change your password at any time in your [Account Settings](/hub/ui/account-settings/).

## Logging In

<Steps>
1. Navigate to the Hub URL in your browser. The login page opens automatically.

2. Enter your **email address** and **password** in the respective fields.

3. Click **Sign in**.
</Steps>

## Forgot Your Password?

Click **Forgot password?** on the login page. Enter your email address and you will receive a reset link. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact your administrator.

## Session and Logout

Hub automatically logs you out after a period of inactivity. On shared or public computers, always log out manually — click your profile icon in the top right and select **Logout**.

## User Management

User management is handled by administrators. They can invite new team members, assign roles, and manage existing accounts. See [Invite a New User](/hub/how-to/invite-user/) for step-by-step instructions.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Login page does not open | Check the Hub URL and your network connection |
| "Invalid credentials" error | Verify your email address and password. Passwords are case-sensitive. |
| No invitation email received | Check your spam folder or ask your administrator to resend the invite |
| Password reset email not received | Check your spam folder or contact your administrator |
| Account locked after multiple failed attempts | Contact your administrator to reset your account |
