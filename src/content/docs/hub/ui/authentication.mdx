## Account Setup

Accounts are created by an admin. Your login credentials (email address and password) are then sent to you manually by the super admin. You can change your password at any time in your [account settings](/hub/ui/account-settings/).

## Logging In

<Steps>
1. Navigate to the Hub URL in your browser. The login page opens automatically.

2. Enter your **E-Mail** address and **Passwort** in the respective fields.

3. Click **Anmelden** to sign in.
</Steps>

</div>

<div class="qs-tip">Forgot your password? Click <strong>Passwort vergessen?</strong> on the login page. Enter your email address and you will receive a reset link.</div>

## Session and Logout

Hub automatically logs you out after a period of inactivity. On shared or public computers, always log out manually by clicking your profile avatar in the top right and selecting **Logout**.

## User Roles

Every Hub account is assigned one of two roles:

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **User** | View devices, software releases, and account settings |
| **Admin** | All User permissions, plus user management (invite, assign roles, deactivate) |

## User Management

User management is handled by administrators. They can invite new team members, assign roles, and manage existing accounts. See [Invite a New User](/hub/how-to/invite-user/) for step-by-step instructions.
