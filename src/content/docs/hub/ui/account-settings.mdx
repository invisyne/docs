The Account Settings page lets you update your name and password.

## Opening Account Settings

Click the **initials avatar** in the top-right corner of the navigation bar to open the user menu.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={dropdownImg.src} alt="Hub user menu dropdown showing Kontoeinstellungen and Logout options" />
</div>

Select **Kontoeinstellungen** to navigate to the settings page.

## Overview

The settings page has two sections — **Persönliche Daten** (personal data) and **Sicherheit** (security).

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={overviewImg.src} alt="Hub Kontoeinstellungen overview with Persönliche Daten and Sicherheit sections" />
</div>

## Persönliche Daten

This section shows your current email address, first name, and last name. The email address is display-only and cannot be changed here.

<Steps>
1. Click **Persönliche Daten bearbeiten** to switch the section into edit mode.

2. Update **Vorname** (first name) and/or **Nachname** (last name) as needed.

3. Click **Persönliche Daten speichern** to apply your changes, or **Abbrechen** to discard them.
</Steps>

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={editPersonalImg.src} alt="Hub personal data form in edit mode showing email, first name, and last name fields" />
</div>

## Sicherheit

The Security section lets you set a new password.

<Steps>
1. Click **Passwort bearbeiten** to open the password form.

2. Enter your **Aktuelles Passwort** (current password), then your new password twice — in **Neues Passwort** and **Neues Passwort wiederholen**.

3. Click **Passwort speichern** to save, or **Abbrechen** to cancel.
</Steps>
