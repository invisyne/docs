The Account Settings page lets you update your name and password.

## Opening Account Settings

Click the **initials avatar** in the top-right corner of the navigation bar to open the user menu. Select **Account Settings** to navigate to the settings page.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={dropdownImg.src} alt="Hub user menu dropdown showing Account Settings and Logout options" />
</div>

## Overview

The settings page has two sections — **Personal Data** and **Security**.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={overviewImg.src} alt="Hub Account Settings overview with Personal Data and Security sections" />
</div>

## Personal Data

This section shows your current email address, first name, and last name. The email address is display-only and cannot be changed here.

<Steps>
1. Click **Edit Personal Data** to switch the section into edit mode.

2. Update your first name and/or last name as needed.

3. Click **Save Personal Data** to apply your changes, or **Cancel** to discard them.
</Steps>

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={editPersonalImg.src} alt="Hub personal data form in edit mode showing email, first name, and last name fields" />
</div>

## Security

The Security section lets you set a new password.

<Steps>
1. Click **Edit Password** to open the password form.

2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice in the fields provided.

3. Click **Save Password** to save, or **Cancel** to cancel.
</Steps>
