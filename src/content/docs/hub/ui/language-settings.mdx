Hub is available in **English** and **German**. You can switch the language at any time using the language selector in the top-right corner.

<div class="qs-tip">The language selection applies to the current browser session only. It is not tied to your account and will reset on the next login.</div>

## Switching the Language

<Steps>
1. Click the language button (e.g., **DE** or **EN**) in the top-right corner of the interface.
2. A dropdown appears with the available languages.
3. Select your preferred language — the interface switches immediately.
</Steps>

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src={langImg.src} alt="Language dropdown open showing available languages" />
</div>

The language change takes effect right away across all menus, labels, form fields, and messages.
