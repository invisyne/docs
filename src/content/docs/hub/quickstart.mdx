Get up and running with Crawler.Hub in minutes. All you need are your login credentials — your account is created by an admin and the credentials are sent to you manually by the super admin.

<div class="qs-stepper">

  <div class="qs-panels">

    <div class="qs-panel" id="qs-0">
      <div class="qs-step-label">Step 1</div>
      <h2 class="qs-title">Log in</h2>
      <p>Navigate to the Hub URL in your browser. Enter your email address and password, then click <strong>Log in</strong>.</p>
      <div class="qs-tip">Forgot your password? Click <strong>Forgot password?</strong> on the login page to reset it via email.</div>
      <div class="screenshot-frame">
        <img src="/images/hub/hub-login.png" alt="Hub Login" />
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="qs-panel" id="qs-1">
      <div class="qs-step-label">Step 2</div>
      <h2 class="qs-title">Devices</h2>
      <p>After logging in you will see the <strong>Devices</strong> section — a table of all registered Edge devices in your organization. Click any row to open the detail view where you can view and edit device information.</p>
      <div class="screenshot-frame">
        <img src="/images/hub/hub-devices.png" alt="Hub Device Overview" />
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="qs-panel" id="qs-2">
      <div class="qs-step-label">Step 3</div>
      <h2 class="qs-title">Software</h2>
      <p>Navigate to <strong>Software</strong> in the top bar to browse current firmware and Companion App releases. Each version shows what's new — download links are available for released versions.</p>
      <div class="screenshot-frame">
        <img src="/images/hub/hub-software.png" alt="Hub Software" />
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="qs-panel" id="qs-3">
      <div class="qs-step-label">Step 4</div>
      <h2 class="qs-title">Account Settings</h2>
      <p>Click your profile icon in the top right corner and select <strong>Account Settings</strong> to update your name or password.</p>
      <div class="screenshot-frame">
        <img src={accountSettingsImg.src} alt="Hub Account Settings showing email address as read-only" />
      </div>
    </div>

  </div>
