The Hub is a central management platform for crawler devices, designed as a “Crawler’s Toolbox.” It serves as a command center for the seamless control and management of all connected crawler devices. The system provides a comprehensive overview of all devices, including their status, performance data, and configurations or software versions.

### Main Purpose

The Hub enables technicians and administrators to centrally manage crawler devices. Users are granted access to the Hub upon request and can log in to obtain an overview of all products related to crawlers. In addition, users can view software statuses and different versions of devices as well as specific accompanying software, including the Crawler Companion App.
