  <div class="page-hero-text">
    <p class="page-hero-lead">Crawler.Hub is the central web-based management portal for registered Edge devices. It gives teams a single access point to monitor all deployed devices, track firmware versions, and retrieve software downloads — without requiring physical or network access to individual devices.</p>
    <div class="page-hero-body">
      <p>Hub is designed for organizations operating multiple Edge units across different sites. Rather than managing each device in isolation, Hub aggregates everything relevant in one place: device status, installed firmware, location, and metadata. Software releases — both Edge firmware and Companion App updates — are published directly in Hub, making it the authoritative source for deployment-relevant downloads.</p>
      <p>Access is governed by roles. Each user is assigned either the <strong>User</strong> or <strong>Admin</strong> role. Users can view devices and access software; Admins additionally manage user accounts and invitations for their organization.</p>
    </div>

## Functional Scope

<div class="role-badge">User</div>

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-card-title">Devices</div>
    <p>Overview of all registered Edge devices with key information such as serial number and firmware version. Click any device to open the detail view.<sup>*</sup></p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-card-title">Software</div>
    <p>Browse current and previous versions of Edge and Companion firmware. Each version lists new features along with links to documentation, changelog, and download.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-card-title">Account Settings</div>
    <p>Edit your personal details (name) and change your password.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="feature-footnote">* Device information such as notes can be edited in the detail view.</p>

<div class="role-badge">Admin</div>

<div class="feature-grid one-col">
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-card-title">User Management</div>
    <p>Invite users by email, assign roles (Admin or User), and manage access within the organization.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="feature-footnote">The Admin role includes all features available to Users.</p>

## Security Notes

<div class="security-grid">
  <div class="security-item">
    <div class="security-item-title">Session Timeout</div>
    <p>Hub automatically logs you out after a period of inactivity. Always log out manually on shared computers.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="security-item">
    <div class="security-item-title">Account Setup</div>
    <p>An admin creates your account and sends you your login credentials. You can change your password at any time.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="security-item">
    <div class="security-item-title">Role-Based Access</div>
    <p>Your access is limited to the functions assigned to your role.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="security-item">
    <div class="security-item-title">HTTPS Only</div>
    <p>Hub is accessible exclusively via an encrypted HTTPS connection.</p>
  </div>
</div>
