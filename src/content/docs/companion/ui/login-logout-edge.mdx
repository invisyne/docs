---
title: "Login to Edge Device"
description: "How to sign in and out of an Edge device in Crawler.Companion."
sidebar:
  order: 3.5
---

Some actions on an Edge device — such as changing settings — require authentication with the device's own credentials. This login is separate from the Hub login and applies per device.

<div class="qs-stepper">

  <div class="qs-tabbar">
    <a class="qs-tab" href="#">Sign in</a>
    <a class="qs-tab" href="#">Sign out</a>
  </div>

  <div class="qs-panels">

    <div class="qs-panel">

      Open the detail panel of a device and click **Erweiterte Einstellungen**. A login prompt appears. Enter the device credentials and confirm.

      <div class="screenshot-frame" style="max-width: 420px">
        <img src="/images/companion/edge-anmelden.png" alt="Crawler.Companion — Edge device login prompt" />
      </div>

    </div>

    <div class="qs-panel">

      Device sessions are tied to the current Companion session. Closing and reopening Companion signs you out of all device sessions automatically.

      <div class="screenshot-frame">
        <img src="/images/companion/edge-abmelden.png" alt="Crawler.Companion — Edge device logout" />
      </div>

    </div>

  </div>

</div>
