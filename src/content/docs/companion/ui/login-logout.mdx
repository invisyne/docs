---
title: "Login / Logout (Hub)"
description: "How to sign in and out of your Hub account in Crawler.Companion, and what the Hub connection unlocks."
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

Crawler.Companion can be used without a Hub account to manage devices on the local network. Signing in with a Hub account additionally enables **Deepview** exports.

<div class="qs-stepper">

  <div class="qs-tabbar">
    <a class="qs-tab" href="#">Sign in</a>
    <a class="qs-tab" href="#">Sign out</a>
  </div>

  <div class="qs-panels">

    <div class="qs-panel">

      Click **Hub-Verbindung** in the top navigation bar. A login dialog appears.

      <div class="screenshot-frame">
        <img src="/images/companion/anmelde-ui.png" alt="Hub login dialog" />
      </div>

      | Field | Description |
      |-------|-------------|
      | **E-Mail** | Your Hub account email address. |
      | **Passwort** | Your Hub account password. |
      | **Angemeldet bleiben** | Keep the session active after closing the app. |

      Click **Anmelden** to sign in. The **Hub-Verbindung** indicator in the header turns green when connected.

      <div class="screenshot-frame" style="max-width: 480px">
        <img src="/images/companion/hub-verbindung.png" alt="Hub connection indicator in the header" />
      </div>

    </div>

    <div class="qs-panel">

      Click the **account icon** (initials) in the top-right corner. A dropdown appears showing the logged-in user. Click **Abmelden** to sign out.

      <div class="screenshot-frame">
        <img src="/images/companion/ausloggen-ui.png" alt="Account menu with Abmelden option" />
      </div>

    </div>

  </div>

</div>
